Informed Consent Form

Welcome to our Global Streetscape Perception Study.

Before you begin, please read the following information carefully. This research is conducted by the University of Tokyo and aims to understand how different people perceive urban environments worldwide.

  • Voluntary Participation: Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
  • Anonymity & Privacy: All data collected will be strictly anonymous. Your personal identity will not be linked to your responses or published in any academic papers.
  • Task Description: You will be asked to compare pairs of street-level images and choose which one better represents a specific attribute (e.g., safety, beauty). Please make your judgments as if you were a pedestrian walking through the scene.
  • Duration: The survey will take approximately 3-5 minutes to complete.

By clicking "I Agree" below, you confirm that you have read and understood the information provided and consent to participate in this study.

Technical note: Images are delivered from Google Drive. If the survey becomes stuck on “Loading Survey” or an image does not appear after several seconds, please refresh this page once before contacting the researcher. After refreshing, click “I Agree” again; your assigned image pair will be restored automatically. Any unsent answers may need to be entered again.
